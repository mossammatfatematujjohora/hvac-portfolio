FAQs

Questions before we start?

Can you track phone calls?

I can configure tracking for clicks on a website phone link. That measures click intent, not a completed call. Actual call tracking may require a dedicated call-tracking setup.

Do you guarantee more leads?

No. Accurate measurement informs decisions, but results depend on the offer, market, campaign, website, budget, and follow-up.

What access might be needed?

Depending on scope, access may be needed to GTM, GA4, Google Ads, the website, or Meta Events Manager. Never share passwords in email.

Is Lone Star Comfort a client?

No. It is a self-initiated demo using a fictional HVAC business to show the setup and testing process.
